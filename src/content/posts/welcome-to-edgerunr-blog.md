What's up Choom!

I wanted a place to write about stuff without tying every post to my offline identity. This blog is intentionally anonymous: the goal is to share useful ideas, experiments, and lessons while keeping the focus on the work rather than on me.

## What I plan to write about

The main focus will be my homelab and the projects around it: self-hosted services, local AI networking, automation, hardware, security, and the occasional problem that took far too long to solve.

Some posts will be polished guides; others will be notes from experiments that might help someone avoid the same dead end.

## Starting small

This is the first post, so the blog is still intentionally simple. I will improve the setup as I use it, document what works, and keep the posts practical.
